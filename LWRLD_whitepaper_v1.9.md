## 4. Tokenomics

### 4.1 Supply

Max supply is one billion LWRLD. On Flap, a new token starts on a bonding curve. Most of the supply sits in the curve until people buy it. When the project reaches Flap's graduation threshold, liquidity is designed to migrate to a DEX pool, commonly PancakeSwap on BNB Chain. Until then, the bonding curve is the market.

There is no team, VC, or advisor allocation and no vesting table. Supply is issued through the launchpad, not a private presale. If that ever changes, this paper will be revised and the change will be dated.

A 9 September 2026 snapshot showed about 5.005 million circulating and the rest still in the curve, with one early buyer holding most of that small float. That was concentration of the float, not a hidden claim on the other supply. Those counts are old. Read the live numbers on Flap.

An early buyer took the first tokens. The 4% tax did not stop that. It only made a fast flip more expensive.

### 4.2 The 4% tax

Every buy and every sell on the taxed pool pays 4%. That 4% is split on-chain:

| Bucket | Share of the 4% | Share of the trade | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Liquidity | 85% | 3.40% | Goes back into the pool |
| Community / project fund | 10% | 0.40% | Project wallet for building, not a silent payday |
| Burn | 5% | 0.20% | Taken out of supply over time |

Worked example. Someone buys $100 of LWRLD on the taxed pool:

- $4.00 is tax.
- $3.40 of that tax is routed toward liquidity.
- $0.40 is routed toward the fund wallet.
- $0.20 is routed toward burn.
- The trader receives roughly $96 of LWRLD at the then-current price, before any other wallet or pool fees.

The fund recipient is:

`0x859B0D91376A6bBF28B1F12F68E916e59708092F`

### 4.3 Why 4%

The tax was chosen to make sniping and dump-and-run expensive. A buy and a sell is about an 8% round trip before slippage. It raises the cost of flipping. It does not stop someone from buying first.

### 4.4 What the tax is not

The fund slice can help pay for streams, art, hosting, or the next feature. It is not a treasury-backed protocol. The burn is not a price guarantee. The tax is not a dividend. Holding $LWRLD does not pay you BNB, and it does not pay you Bitcoin.
